## Runtime requests
There are no external runtime requests. All fonts, icons and scripts are served from this site (assets/, style.css, site.js); there are no CDN links, analytics or tracking. Fonts are self-hosted woff2 files from the npm @fontsource packages; their OFL licence texts are in assets/fonts/. IBM Carbon licence text is in assets/icons/carbon/.
