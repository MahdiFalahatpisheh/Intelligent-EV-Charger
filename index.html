Product

Charging for shared garages.

Our charger and wireless mesh aim to share power in multi-residential garages with no mobile or Wi-Fi signal.

Status: first-revision prototype, not yet tested in a building.

Three parts, one system

  • Charger on a mesh

    Our charger, with control board and wireless-mesh radio built in, joins the mesh.

  • Meters

    Each charger and the whole building are metered.

  • Load management

    The building's available power is shared between chargers. The decision is made locally.

From one charger to a whole garage

Mesh network

A mesh between floors.

Chargers talk over a wireless mesh. It needs no mobile plan and no building Wi-Fi.

Range is not yet proven in a building.

View the technology.

Metering

Know what each uses.

Each charger measures its own power use. The building's total is measured too.

Meter values are reported to the charging back office.

Measurement accuracy is not yet specified.
